Q: What courses are offered at BMSCE?
A: BMS College of Engineering (BMSCE) offers undergraduate B.Tech/B.E. programs, postgraduate M.Tech and MBA/MCA programs, and Ph.D. research programs across various engineering and management disciplines.

Q: What are the library timings at BMSCE?
A: The central library at BMSCE is open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM on weekdays and 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on weekends and public holidays.

Q: Is hostel accommodation available at BMSCE?
A: Yes, BMSCE provides separate, well-equipped hostel facilities for both boys and girls with 24/7 security, Wi-Fi, dining, and recreational amenities.

Q: What is the admission process for BMSCE?
A: Admissions to BMSCE are based on entrance examinations such as KCET, COMEDK, and Management Quota for B.E. courses, and GATE/PGCET/KMAT for postgraduate programs.

Q: Where is BMSCE located?
A: BMS College of Engineering is located at Bull Temple Road, Basavanagudi, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560019.

Q: What are the placement highlights at BMSCE?
A: BMSCE has an outstanding placement cell. Top recruiting companies include Microsoft, Amazon, Cisco, TCS, Infosys, Accenture, and Bosch, offering competitive packages.
